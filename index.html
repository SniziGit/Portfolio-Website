About Me!

As a creative person at heart, I am constantly fascinated by uniqueness and the ingenuity of others. I am always on the lookout for new and avant garde works by others. Be it games and movies or software and projects.

I constantly seek to learn from others to further build my abilities. I am able to pick new skills and software up quickly. In work, I am dedicated to assigned tasks and take pride in my own work. I am receptive to feedback on ideas as well as constructive criticism. I see the need for everyone to be heard in the team in order to foster a resilient team dynamic.

In my free time, I love to play sandbox games, looter-shooters and RPGs and I cycles to stay fit. I also delve into the most mixed bag of passion projects; making artwork, photography, songwriting, videogames and even my own Unity Packages.
